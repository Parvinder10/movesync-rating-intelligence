Prioritization Methodology & Scoring Rationale

Formula & Factor Weights:

Score = (P × 0.3) + (S × 0.3) + (I × 0.2) + (C × 0.1) - (E × 0.1)

  • Prevalence (P): Share of critical (1-2★) reviews in the period normalized [1..10].
  • Severity (S): Physical disruption to passenger commute viability (Stranded: 9.5, Auth block: 8.0).
  • Enterprise Impact (I): B2B SLA breach risk and corporate client contract churn probability.
  • Confidence (C): Statistical reliability proportional to logged sample volume.
  • Effort (E): Estimated engineering and vendor operational overhead to implement fix.
First-Action Defense: Although Driver Conduct (#1) has higher raw complaints, it requires physical fleet governance across hundreds of vendors. ACT-001 (Auth / Rating Decoupling) is a zero-dependency client-side fix with immediate ROI.
